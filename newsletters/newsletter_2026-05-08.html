MONEY TIP

The 3a Loophole Expats Keep Missing

If you're employed in Switzerland but don't have a company pension (hello, freelancers and contractors), you can contribute up to CHF 35,280 to your Pillar 3a in 2026—not just the standard CHF 7,056. That's a potential tax saving of CHF 10,000+ depending on your canton. Zurich residents in the top bracket save roughly 33% on contributions. Open a second 3a account to stagger withdrawals later and avoid a brutal one-time tax hit.
