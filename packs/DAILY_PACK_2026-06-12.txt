**What should expats know about the SNB rate decision timing for transfers?**

Having navigated Swiss banking for over a decade, I've learned one crucial lesson: central bank announcements are not the time to be moving large sums internationally.

Currency volatility spikes 40% around central bank decisions—this isn't theory, it's observable market behavior. The recent EUR/CHF drop of 1.2% might seem small, but on a CHF 100,000 property deposit or university tuition payment, that's CHF 1,200 evaporating in hours.

For the 2026 SNB decisions, here's my approach:

**Plan transfers 2-3 weeks before scheduled announcements.** The SNB calendar is public—use it. Markets price in expectations beforehand, so volatility peaks around the actual decision.

**Consider forward contracts** if you have predictable large transfers (tuition semesters, mortgage payments). Lock rates now while CHF strength is relatively stable.

**Split larger transfers** rather than moving everything at once. I personally use 3-4 tranches for amounts over CHF 50,000.

Summer transfers—family support, property closings, education costs—deserve strategic timing, not reactive panic. The SNB's decisions affect us all, but preparation separates those who lose money from those who don't.
