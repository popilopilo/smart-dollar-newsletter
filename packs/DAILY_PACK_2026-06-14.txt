--- COPY ---
Having lived through the negative interest rate era in Switzerland, I can tell you that 0.75% feels almost luxurious by comparison.

Here's the current landscape for 2026:

**Neobanks leading the charge:**
- Neon: 0.75% up to CHF 25,000
- Yuh: Similar competitive rates with flexible conditions
- Zak: Attractive for bundled banking packages

**Traditional banks:**
Still lagging significantly—most cantonal and major banks offer 0.25-0.4% at best, with UBS and Credit Suisse (now merged) barely moving the needle.

**What Swiss residents should know:**

The SNB has kept rates stable, which means banks aren't under pressure to raise deposit rates aggressively. The competition is primarily coming from neobanks hungry for market share.

My strategy? I split savings across multiple neobanks to maximize the tiered rate benefits. Yes, it's more accounts to manage, but the difference adds up when you're parking emergency funds.

One caveat: always verify deposit protection. Swiss banks cover CHF 100,000 per depositor—neobanks included, as long as they're properly licensed.

After years of literally paying banks to hold our money, even modest positive returns feel like a win.
