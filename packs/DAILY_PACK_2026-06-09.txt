quora.com -> search: Is investing in Swiss francs through pillar 3a a good strategy for 2026?

--- COPY ---
Great question! If you're considering Swiss franc investments for 2026, your pillar 3a should absolutely be part of that strategy.

Here's what most people overlook: the 2026 maximum contribution is CHF 7,056 for employees with a pension fund. That's not just savings—it's an immediate return through tax deduction. Depending on your canton and marginal tax rate, you could save 25-40% on that amount in taxes alone. No investment can guarantee those returns.

My approach as a Swiss resident:

1. **Max out 3a first** - The tax advantage is essentially risk-free yield
2. **Choose a 3a with equity exposure** - If you have 10+ years until retirement, a stock-heavy 3a (like those from VIAC, Finpension, or Frankly) historically outperforms traditional bank 3a accounts
3. **Consider multiple 3a accounts** - You can split contributions across providers for flexibility during withdrawal

June is actually the perfect reminder to set up your contributions if you haven't already. Many people wait until December and either forget or lack the liquidity.

The Swiss franc's stability makes these tax-advantaged accounts even more attractive compared to investing in foreign currencies.
