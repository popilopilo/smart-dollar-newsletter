Here's what most expats and even longtime residents don't realize: Swiss grocery shopping has actually become *cheaper* in 2025-2026, despite what the general cost-of-living complaints suggest.

Migros and Coop have quietly slashed prices on over 1,200 products since April. We're talking meaningful reductions—butter dropped 8%, pasta is down 12%. These aren't promotional gimmicks; they're permanent price adjustments driven by competitive pressure and a stubbornly strong franc making imports cheaper.

My practical tips after 15 years here:

**Stack the savings.** Combine these lower base prices with Cumulus/Supercard points, weekly Aktionen, and the -50% evening stickers on expiring items. I regularly save 25-30% on my weekly shop.

**Cross-border strategically.** With the franc this strong, Constance, Divonne, or Lörrach trips yield even better value on alcohol, meat, and cleaning products.

**Don't sleep on Denner and Aldi.** Their quality has improved dramatically, and prices sit 15-20% below the big two on comparable items.

The irony? Switzerland is getting relatively *more* affordable while neighboring eurozone countries face persistent inflation. Time to adjust your assumptions.
