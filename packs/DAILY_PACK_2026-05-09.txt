If you're among the 370,000+ cross-border workers in Switzerland, you're likely bleeding cash on poor exchange rates. Traditional banks charge spreads of 2-3%, meaning a €5,000 salary conversion could cost you €100-150 monthly in hidden fees alone.

After 8 years managing CHF exposure, here's what actually works:

**Use specialized FX services.** Wise, Revolut, or Interactive Brokers offer spreads of 0.3-0.5%. The savings compound dramatically over time.

**Time your conversions strategically.** The EUR/CHF pair typically sees volatility around SNB announcements and EU economic releases. I personally convert larger amounts when the franc weakens temporarily.

**Consider a multi-currency approach.** Keep operational funds in CHF, but don't convert everything immediately. Having both EUR and CHF accounts gives you flexibility to convert when rates favor you.

**Don't ignore the 3rd pillar.** If you're paying Swiss taxes, maximizing your 3a contributions in CHF-denominated funds reduces taxable income while building franc-based wealth.

**Watch SNB policy closely.** The central bank's intervention history means sudden franc movements aren't uncommon. Stay informed.
