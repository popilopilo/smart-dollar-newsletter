--- COPY ---
The SNB has been aggressively cutting rates throughout 2024-2025, and most analysts expect them to hold steady or potentially dip further by 2026. We're currently sitting at 0.25%, and there's genuine debate about whether we'll see negative rates return.

Here's what's actually happening on the ground: Swiss savings rates have collapsed. We went from 1.5% to under 1% at most banks since March. UBS just dropped to 0.4% this month—painful if you're holding significant CHF.

Neon still offers 0.75% with no strings attached, which is currently one of the better deals for everyday savers.

My take as someone who's watched SNB policy for years: they're prioritizing franc stability over saver returns. The CHF remains stubbornly strong, and the SNB will keep rates low to prevent further appreciation. For 2026, expect rates somewhere between 0% and 0.5%—don't count on meaningful returns from cash holdings.

Practical advice? Diversify beyond CHF savings accounts. Consider Säule 3a for tax benefits, or look at conservative ETF options if you have a longer horizon.
