Having lived in Switzerland for over a decade and watched currency fluctuations closely, I can tell you this is genuinely one of the better moments we've seen for CHF holders traveling to the eurozone.

The Swiss franc has strengthened approximately 8.7% against the euro compared to last year, which translates to meaningful savings on your holiday budget. A trip that would have cost you 2,000 CHF last summer effectively costs you around 1,840 CHF now in purchasing power terms.

My practical advice as someone who regularly crosses into Italy and France:

- **Avoid airport exchange counters** – their margins are brutal, often 5-8% worse than market rates
- **Use a multi-currency card** like Wise or Revolut for close-to-interbank rates
- **Consider exchanging a portion now** if you have confirmed travel plans – currency markets can reverse quickly

The SNB hasn't shown aggressive intervention intentions lately, but geopolitical events or ECB policy shifts could change the landscape rapidly. The franc tends to strengthen during uncertainty, but nothing is guaranteed.
