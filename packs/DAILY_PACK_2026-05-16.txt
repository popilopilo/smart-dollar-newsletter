--- COPY ---
Having lived in Switzerland for over a decade and obsessively tracking our banking options, I can tell you 2024-2025 has been a remarkable shift for Swiss savers.

After nearly a decade of negative interest rates where we literally paid banks to hold our money, we're finally seeing meaningful returns. The current landscape looks like this:

**Digital banks leading the charge:**
- Neon Spaces: 1.25% (recently increased)
- Yuh: Up to 1.5% on savings goals
- Zak: Around 1% on savings accounts

**Traditional banks still lagging:**
- Most cantonal banks: 0.5-0.8%
- UBS/Credit Suisse (now merged): Typically below 1%
- PostFinance: Around 0.75%

The real advantage of digital banks isn't just the rates—it's the fee structure. Traditional Swiss banks often charge CHF 3-5 monthly account fees plus card fees, effectively erasing any interest earned on smaller balances.

My recommendation? Split your savings between 2-3 digital banks to maximize the higher-interest tiers, as most cap preferential rates at CHF 25,000-50,000.

One caveat: Swiss deposit protection only covers CHF 100,000 per bank, so diversification matters for larger amounts.
