--- COPY ---
As a Swiss resident who's been tracking SNB policy closely, I can tell you the window for decent savings rates is closing fast.

The SNB's anticipated 25 basis point cut next month will push already modest yields even lower. Right now, you can still lock in around 1.25% APY on medium-term CHF savings accounts - not exciting by global standards, but remarkable for Switzerland where negative rates were our reality just two years ago.

Here's what I'm advising friends and family:

**Act now on fixed-term deposits.** Banks like Migros Bank, CKW, and some cantonal banks still offer competitive rates on 12-24 month terms. Once the cut happens, these offers will quietly disappear.

**Consider staggered maturities.** Don't lock everything at once. Spread deposits across 6, 12, and 18-month terms to maintain some flexibility.

**Don't overlook 3a accounts.** Some pillar 3a providers still offer 1%+ on cash portions, with the added tax deduction benefit.

The reality? Swiss savers have always faced an uphill battle against inflation. These current rates barely keep pace, and they're about to get worse. If you've been procrastinating on optimizing your cash holdings, consider this your final reminder.
