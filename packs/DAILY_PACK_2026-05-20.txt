The SNB held rates steady at 0.25% in March 2025, and most analysts expect another hold at the June 2025 meeting. Looking ahead to May 2026 is genuinely speculative territory—we're talking about a decision nearly a year out.

That said, the trajectory matters for your planning. The SNB has been cutting aggressively (from 1.75% down to 0.25% over 2024), and unless inflation resurges significantly, we're likely looking at a low-rate environment for the foreseeable future.

For Swiss residents, this means savings account yields will remain compressed. Currently, Neon offers 1.0% on flexible savings, while some tied accounts push slightly higher. By May 2026? Expect similar or potentially lower rates if the SNB cuts further.

My practical take: don't obsess over rate predictions 12+ months out. Instead, focus on what you can control—minimizing fees, maintaining emergency liquidity, and considering whether pillar 3a contributions make more sense for longer-term savings given tax advantages.

The SNB prioritizes price stability and currency management over savers' returns. Plan accordingly rather than hoping for rate normalization.
