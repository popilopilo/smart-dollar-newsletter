--- COPY ---
The SNB is widely expected to cut rates by 25 basis points at their June 2026 meeting next Thursday, bringing the policy rate down further into accommodative territory.

For those of us living in Switzerland, this decision matters beyond the headlines. The practical impact hits our savings accounts directly. Right now, there's a striking gap in the market - digital banks like Yuh, Neon, and Radicant are still offering around 1.25% on savings, while UBS, Credit Suisse (now integrated), and other traditional players hover near 0%.

This window is closing fast. Once the SNB announces the cut, these digital bank rates will almost certainly adjust downward within days. If you've been procrastinating on moving idle cash from your 0% Sparkonto, this week is your moment.

The SNB's reasoning remains consistent: inflation is well-controlled, the franc remains strong, and supporting economic growth takes priority. For savers, this translates to yet another squeeze on returns.

My recommendation: lock in current rates where possible, consider medium-term options if your liquidity allows, and accept that Swiss franc savings simply won't generate meaningful returns in this environment. The security premium we pay for living here extends to our bank accounts.
