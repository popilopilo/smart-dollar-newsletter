**What are current Swiss savings rates in 2026, and what should I do?**

Honestly? It's pretty grim right now if you're parking cash in a standard savings account.

UBS just dropped their rate to 0.65% last week, which stung. Raiffeisen is slightly better at 0.75%, but let's be real—neither is keeping pace with even our modest inflation. PostFinance and ZKB are hovering in similar territory.

Here's what most Swiss residents don't realize: with the SNB signaling another potential cut in June, these rates will likely drop further. We could be looking at 0.5% or lower by autumn.

**My recommendation as someone who's navigated Swiss banking for 12+ years:**

Lock in a 12-month fixed-term deposit NOW. Several cantonal banks are still offering 1.1-1.3% if you commit today. Yes, your money is tied up, but you're essentially getting double the return versus a flexible savings account.

Also worth considering: Säule 3a contributions before year-end. Tax deduction plus slightly better rates—it's the closest thing to free money in Switzerland.

Don't let your savings erode while waiting for "better timing." In Swiss banking, the best rate is usually the one you locked in yesterday.
