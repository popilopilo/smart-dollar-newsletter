What does this mean practically?

**For mortgage holders:** Variable rate mortgages (SARON-based) will stay affordable for now. If you locked in a fixed rate during the negative interest era, you're sitting pretty. Those renewing soon should compare offers carefully—banks have room to compete.

**For savers:** Don't expect savings account rates to improve. The 0.5-1% you're getting is likely the ceiling for this cycle. Consider Säule 3a contributions before year-end if you haven't maxed out.

**For the CHF:** Expect continued strength. The SNB isn't uncomfortable with a strong franc when inflation is this tame—it actually helps keep import prices down.

**My take:** This pause suggests the SNB sees no urgency in either direction. They'll likely wait until Q1 2025 to reassess, watching how the eurozone evolves.
