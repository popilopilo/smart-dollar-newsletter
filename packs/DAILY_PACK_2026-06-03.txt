Living in Switzerland, I've been tracking this closely, and honestly, the window for decent savings rates is closing fast.

Right now, you can still find 1.25-1.5% APY on Swiss franc savings accounts, which sounds modest but is actually remarkable given Switzerland's history of negative rates. Neon just extended their 1.25% promotional rate through August 2025, which is worth noting if you've been sitting on the fence.

Here's the reality check though: the SNB is currently at 0.75% and there's growing speculation about another cut. When that happens—and most analysts think it's a matter of when, not if—banks will follow suit within weeks. We saw this pattern after every recent SNB decision.

My approach? I've moved idle cash into these promotional accounts now rather than waiting. The difference between earning 1.25% and 0.5% on 50,000 CHF is roughly 375 CHF annually—not life-changing, but not nothing either.

The Swiss banking landscape rewards those who actually pay attention and act. Most people leave money in accounts earning 0.1% because switching feels like effort. Don't be most people.
