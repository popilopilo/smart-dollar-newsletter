Living in Switzerland, I can confirm this is genuinely exciting news for savers here.

With inflation at just 0.8% and savings accounts now offering rates between 1-1.5% at most major banks, we're experiencing something that felt impossible just two years ago: actual purchasing power growth on idle cash.

Here's the practical reality for 2026 planning:

**What's working:**
- Medium-term savings accounts (12-24 months) at cantonal banks are offering the best rates
- Pillar 3a accounts remain tax-advantaged and now provide meaningful real returns
- The strong franc adds another layer of wealth preservation

**What I'm doing personally:**
- Keeping 6 months expenses in a high-yield savings account
- Maximizing Pillar 3a contributions before year-end
- Not rushing into riskier investments just for yield

The SNB's steady hand at 1.25% suggests this environment continues into 2026. Unlike our eurozone neighbors still battling higher inflation, Swiss residents can actually build wealth through simple savings vehicles.

One caveat: rates vary significantly between banks. UBS and Credit Suisse successors often lag behind cantonal banks and online alternatives like Yuh or Neon.
