Having navigated the Swiss mortgage market for over 15 years, I can tell you 2026 is shaping up to be genuinely interesting for borrowers.

The numbers speak for themselves: 10-year fixed mortgages are now averaging 1.89% across major Swiss lenders, down significantly from 2.4% in January. This represents one of the most favorable rate environments we've seen since 2022.

For context, on a CHF 800,000 mortgage (fairly standard for the Swiss market), this rate drop translates to approximately CHF 4,200 in annual savings. That's not insignificant, especially compounded over a 10-year term.

My practical advice for those up for renewal in the next 12 months: seriously consider locking in now. The SNB's monetary policy trajectory suggests rates could stabilize here, but geopolitical uncertainties and inflation dynamics in the Eurozone create unpredictable pressures.

A few things Swiss residents often overlook:
- Compare Kantonalbanken rates against private lenders
- Negotiate – Swiss banks have more flexibility than they initially reveal
- Consider splitting between fixed and SARON-linked tranches for flexibility

Don't wait until your renewal deadline. Start conversations with lenders 6 months ahead.
